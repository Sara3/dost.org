2024 / Yearly update

Support from friends and family

A private campaign brought together direct contributions from friends and family to support people in need.

About this record

In 2024, Dost organized a private fundraising campaign within its circle of friends and family. Supporters contributed directly, and the funds were distributed to people in need.

The campaign was organized internally, without a public fundraising appeal. Because contributions came directly from friends and family, Dost did not publish a public annual report at the time.

This update has been added to the archive to document that year’s work and explain how the campaign was supported.

Retrospective update, added in September 2026 based on the account provided by Dost. No fundraising amount or recipient count is stated in this record.
