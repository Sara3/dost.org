2026 Ramadan appeal

Dost’s annual appeal supporting food assistance, medical expenses and education costs for families in Afghanistan.

March 142026 San Francisco gathering

About this record

The 2026 Ramadan appeal brought supporters together in San Francisco and online. The community iftar took place on March 14.

The appeal supports Dost’s work with families in Afghanistan. Local contacts help coordinate food and financial assistance according to household needs.

Earlier distribution reports describe how previous contributions were used. The latest available distribution report covers 2025 and was published in February 2026.

A separate account of how the 2026 funds were distributed has not yet been added to this archive.
