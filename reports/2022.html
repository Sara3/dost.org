2022 / Distribution report

Food assistance for 110 families in Daikundi

An April 2022 field update documents food packages for 110 families in Daikundi, Afghanistan.

110Families reported supported
DaikundiDistribution location
April 12022 field update

Report summary

Ahead of Ramadan in 2022, Sara Daqiq collected contributions from friends and family and sent $6,000 to Afghanistan. Her Facebook update, dated April 1, records that the funds had been sent for distribution.

The field report shared by Zia Arman on the same date describes food assistance for 110 families in Daikundi. It lists flour, rice and cooking oil as the contents of the household packages. The photographs document people gathered with supplies at the distribution site.

The post describes support for households facing severe financial hardship. Contributions collected abroad were turned into essential food supplies and distributed locally.

What was provided

  • Flour for household meals
  • Rice as a staple food
  • Cooking oil; the field post notes that the oil was not shown in the photographs

Prepared from the dated Facebook record supplied by Dost. The English summary also draws on the accompanying Dari field report. The $6,000 is the reported amount sent; the post does not provide a complete expense reconciliation.
