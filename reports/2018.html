2018 / Distribution report

Food and cash support for eight families

Food packages and cash assistance for eight families, documented in a follow-up published in 2019.

$1,300Reported distributed
8Families supported

Report summary

The report looks back on a 2018 fundraiser and describes food packages for six families, followed by cash assistance for two additional families in a remote area.

Its closing summary reports $1,300 distributed among eight families. Food packages included staples such as rice, flour, oil, and macaroni, with deliveries coordinated by relatives in Afghanistan.

This is the earliest distribution documented by the public reports currently linked here. It does not establish the organization’s legal incorporation date.

The original online appeal

A Facebook post dated June 4, 2018 shared the early GoFundMe appeal, titled “Gift Eidy to four needy families,” and described increasing the goal to include more families. The later distribution account linked below reports assistance for eight families.

The work took place in 2018; the report was published in January 2019.
