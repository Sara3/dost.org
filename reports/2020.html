2020 / Distribution report

Food packages in Shahristan, cash support in Kabul

Care packages in Shahristan and cash assistance in Kabul during the COVID-19 lockdown.

$5,000Sent to Shahristan
$1,805.09Cash support in Kabul
44Households in care-package account

Report summary

The report describes sending $5,000 to a trusted shopkeeper in Shahristan district for care packages. The planned support was adjusted to approximately $114 per household so that more families could be included.

A further $1,805.09 was distributed as cash assistance in Kabul. The report explains that photographs from Kabul could not be obtained during the COVID-19 lockdown.

The care-package account describes 44 households and estimates at least 308 people supported, based on seven people per household. That person count is an estimate, not an individually verified total.

The two reported distribution amounts total $6,805.09. The estimated people count is not used as a cumulative impact claim.
