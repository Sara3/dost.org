2021 / Distribution report

Food and basic necessities for 80 families

A May 2021 follow-up records completed assistance for 80 families in Daikundi, following the Ramadan appeal.

80Families reported supported
DaikundiDistribution location
May 282021 completion update

Report summary

On May 28, 2021, Sara Daqiq reported that donations had helped provide basic necessities to 80 families. The update was accompanied by photographs of households gathered with food packages.

The field post shared by Zia Arman on the same date identifies the distribution area as Miramor district in Daikundi province. It describes packages containing flour, rice and cooking oil.

An earlier appeal, published on May 9, had proposed support for 60 families. The later Facebook update records the completed distribution to 80 families. This report uses the follow-up for the household count and keeps the earlier plan as background.

From appeal to distribution

  • May 9: the fundraising appeal set out a plan for 60 families.
  • May 28: the organizer reported that 80 families received basic necessities, supported by a field update and photographs.

The completed household count comes from the May 28 Facebook update supplied by Dost. The earlier budget was a plan and is not presented as a final account of expenditure.
