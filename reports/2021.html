2021 / Fundraising update

Food packages for 60 families: the fundraising plan

A Ramadan appeal outlining a food-package plan for 60 families, coordinated with relatives in Afghanistan.

60Families in the plan
7,600 AFNPlanned package cost
456,000 AFNPlanned package budget

Report summary

The 2021 appeal set out a plan to provide food packages for 60 families. At 7,600 Afghan afghanis per package, the planned food budget was 456,000 AFN, described in the source as about $5,930.

Distribution was to be coordinated by the organizer’s cousin while the family was traveling. The appeal also identified additional delivery and transfer costs.

The source is a fundraising plan, not a completed distribution reconciliation. The 60-family figure is a target and is not included as a verified outcome.

Planned amounts and family counts should not be read as final distributions.

Original source · Published May 9, 2021
