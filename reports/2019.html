Report summary

The organizer reported receiving $931.59 from GoFundMe after platform fees, plus a $100 family contribution, for $1,031.59 available before the reported $8 transfer fee.

Support combined food packages and cash according to household needs. When two intended recipients could not be reached, the report says families on a waiting list received support instead.

The original report lists individual allocations. This summary does not infer a final household count or a reconciled distribution total from the incomplete replacement-family details.

Funds received are distinct from the amount ultimately distributed. See the original report for the listed allocations.
