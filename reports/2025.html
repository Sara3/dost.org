Report summary

In 2025, the community raised $5,226. The report describes support for seven families in Afghanistan, with needs ranging from medical treatment and ongoing medication to education, food, and basic household expenses.

Approximately $600 in event ingredient costs and $130 in platform fees were paid from donations, leaving approximately $4,496 for family support. The organizers covered the venue, supplies, most ingredients, and money-transfer costs themselves.

The report does not provide exact allocations per family because support included ongoing treatment and medication. The figures here preserve that distinction rather than implying a complete family-by-family accounting.

This summary covers the 2025 distribution, although the source was published in February 2026. Amounts marked ≈ are approximate.
